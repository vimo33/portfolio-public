Take Me There

3-wall AI portal installation

An immersive portal that uses AI to transport people into places they've never been — real places, rendered in real time.

Watch trailer View repo
Role
Creative Technologist

Problem

Travel is limited by time, cost, and access. Existing media can't replicate the feeling of truly being somewhere.

Approach

Combine generative AI, spatial audio, and immersive projection to create a believable sense of place.

System

Real-time environment generation with vision models, depth mapping, and context-aware interaction.

Outcomes

A transportive experience that evokes wonder, presence, and emotional connection to the world.
