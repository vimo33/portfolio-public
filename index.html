03 / THE PERSON

A designer’s eye.
A founder’s itch.
A beginner’s mind.

I’m Vikas Mohan, based in Zurich. My path runs through creative direction, digital strategy, entrepreneurship, and now a growing practice of building with AI.

I’m drawn to the space between “what if?” and “try this.” I shape the problem, design the experience, and work with AI coding tools and collaborators to make the idea tangible.

At Bildspur, that includes immersive light and the tools behind our studio. With Maurus at Follow the Rabbit, it includes client pilots and business workflows. My independent explorations connect memory, local infrastructure, sound and social experiences.

THE LONGER THREADVENTURES / COLLABORATIONS / PRACTICE

A few chapters.
One curious mind.

From creative direction and digital strategy to practical tools and immersive experiences. Different settings, the same urge to make an idea tangible.

CURRENT COLLABORATION / LIGHT & STUDIO TOOLS

Bildspur

My work with Bildspur connects immersive light experiences with the tools that make them possible. I have worked extensively on the LED Forest app, alongside Inventory OS, which we use to explore almost 2,000 items across locations and plan builds with an agent canvas. The wider work includes a 3D Structure Builder, a shared knowledge and task surface, and a design system: practical software that supports making things together.

COLLABORATION WITH MAURUS / CLIENT WORKFLOWS

Follow the Rabbit Studio

With Maurus, I build tools around real workflows. I have led most of the work on the RTW quoting pilot, which we are currently implementing, and built a sales workflow that researches relevant companies and brings them into our custom Kanban process. My focus is on making the journey from information to a useful next action clearer.

FOUNDER & COMMUNITY CATALYST / 2023–PRESENT

Samuha

With Samuha, I turn ideas into practical products using no-code tools and open-source platforms. For Falconi Consulting, I led UX strategy and design workshops with its team and selected clients, shaping clearer onboarding workflows and bringing content and communication together. Samuha remains active, with reduced focus while my priorities have shifted to other work.

FOUNDER / 2019–2023 / ZURICH

Fixt GmbH

I founded Fixt to help businesses solve problems through design and technology. For Plentii.ch, a crowdsourcing and crowdfunding platform for NGOs, I led technology strategy, development and delivery, working with its design agency to connect the product vision with what could be built. The work moved from an MVP launch into two and a half years of continued development.

FOUNDER & DIGITAL STRATEGY / 2011–2024 / BANGALORE

Tiramisu New Media Solutions

At Tiramisu, I combined founding a business with digital strategy and design work for clients. My focus was on bringing thoughtful design and UX into digital communication, alongside product development, brand awareness and campaigns across channels. It was a sustained practice of understanding a client need and translating it into a useful digital experience.

CREATIVE LEAD / 2007–2011 / BANGALORE

ADventure Advertising Solutions

I worked on key accounts and managed client deliveries across branding, web design and development. This was the foundation of my practice: understanding what a client needed, shaping the creative response and carrying it through to delivery.

COLLABORATION / RESEARCH TOOLS

Bakboka

With Bakboka, I explore research tools that make complex domains easier to navigate: MPS connects climate, food and debt evidence; Tourism Risk Monitor organises regional signals; Multiplium structures regenerative-viticulture research. The thread is moving from a broad question to sources and assumptions someone can inspect.

HOW I WORK
